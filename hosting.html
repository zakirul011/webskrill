Good to know

Hosting questions, answered.

What is shared web hosting?

Shared hosting places your website on a server alongside other hosting accounts. It is a practical, budget-friendly way to publish a personal site, portfolio or small business website without managing a server yourself.

What does BDIX hosting mean?

BDIX is the Bangladesh Internet Exchange. A BDIX-connected hosting plan is intended to provide an efficient route for visitors accessing your website from participating local networks.

Which plan should I choose?

Mini suits a single small site. Choose Pro or Max if you need to host more websites or need additional storage, bandwidth, email accounts or databases. Max Plus provides the largest listed allocations. Check your expected usage before ordering.

Are the listed prices monthly or yearly?

The prices on this page are the annual amounts shown in the WebSkrill billing catalogue, in USD. Confirm the current price, currency and available billing terms on the checkout page before placing an order.

Can I host more than one website?

Yes. The listed website limits range from one site on Mini to six on Max Plus. Choose a plan that meets your needs, and confirm the latest account limits with the provider if you are unsure.

Where can I get help choosing a plan?

Contact WebSkrill through the support page or message the team on WhatsApp before ordering.
